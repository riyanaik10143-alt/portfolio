04 — Design Process

From brief
to delivery

Every project runs through the same five stages, so the outcome is intentional — never accidental.

01

Research

Audit the brand, its competitors and its audience. Build moodboards, collect references and lock the visual territory before a single pixel moves.

02

Sketch

Fast thumbnails and hand sketches to explore composition, type weight and layout tension — cheap iterations, honest decisions.

03

Digital Design

Build the chosen direction on a strict grid in Canva, Photoshop or Illustrator: type system, spacing scale, tonal contrast and export-ready structure.

04

Mockups

Place the design into real-world context — tees, tags, packaging, feeds and banners — to test how it survives outside the artboard.

05

Final Delivery

Clean files, correct sizes, print and social variants, plus a short usage guide so the brand stays consistent after handover.
